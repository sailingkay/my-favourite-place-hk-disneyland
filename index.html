Hong Kong Disneyland is my favourite place. It is in Penny's Bay on Lantau Island and it opened on 12 September 2005. Today the park has eight themed lands. This website introduces the park: its history, its lands, and some useful visiting tips.

Let's start the magical tour!　一齊開始奇妙之旅！
